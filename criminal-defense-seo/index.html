Questions Answered

Why does ad scheduling matter so much for criminal defense firms?

Arrests and criminal charges happen at all hours, often overnight or on weekends, and the searcher frequently needs representation immediately. A firm that is only visible during business hours misses a significant share of the highest urgency, highest intent searches in this practice area.

How is criminal defense SEO different from personal injury SEO?

Both involve urgent decisions, but criminal defense searches skew more heavily toward mobile devices at unusual hours, often from someone other than the accused, such as a family member searching on their behalf. Content and ad strategy need to account for that searcher being a different person than the eventual client.

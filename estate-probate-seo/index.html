Questions Answered

Why do estate and probate clients prefer local firms?

Estate and probate matters often involve sensitive family dynamics and original documents. Clients overwhelmingly prefer a firm they can visit in person rather than one they only interact with remotely, which makes local SEO and Google Maps visibility especially important for this practice area.

Is estate planning content different from probate content?

Significantly. Estate planning clients are in a calm, forward planning mindset and research thoroughly before choosing a firm. Probate clients are often under time pressure and emotional strain. Content that works for one audience feels jarring or irrelevant to the other.

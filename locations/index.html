Why this matters

Why Each City Needs
Its Own Strategy

📱

Search behaviour differs

Lagos legal searches happen overwhelmingly on mobile, often on slower connections. London searches skew desktop with longer research sessions. Melbourne searches trend highly local by suburb. One strategy cannot serve all three.

🏛️

Legal frameworks are different

Victoria's family violence intervention orders, Delaware's Court of Chancery, London's SRA advertising rules, Dubai's DIFC jurisdiction, and Nigeria's compensation law all require content that reflects the specific jurisdiction rather than generic legal copy.

🌐

Language and audience vary

Dubai requires bilingual English and Arabic content. London serves a linguistically diverse immigration client base. Lagos content must load fast enough to retain visitors on 3G connections. Each audience has different expectations and constraints.

⚔️

Competition levels differ

London's legal market has decades of digital authority built up by established firms. Lagos has significant opportunities for firms willing to invest in mobile performance. Delaware rewards national keyword reach alongside local presence. Each requires a different entry approach.

🗺️

Local signals are city specific

The directories that carry citation weight, the publications that produce valuable backlinks, and the review platforms clients actually use all vary by market. A citation strategy built for London will not move the needle in Lagos.

🔒

One firm per practice per city

We do not take on competing firms in the same practice area and city. If we are already working with a personal injury firm in Lagos, we will not take another. Your strategy is built to help you win, not split the market between our own clients.

Questions Answered

Why does each city need a different SEO strategy?

Legal search behaviour, competitive landscapes, and local ranking signals all differ meaningfully between cities and countries. A strategy built for London, where E-E-A-T authority and editorial backlinks dominate, will underperform in Lagos, where mobile page speed and 3G optimisation are the deciding factors. We build each city strategy from the ground up rather than applying a single template across all markets.

Do you work with law firms outside these five cities?

Currently we operate in Lagos, Delaware, London, Dubai, and Melbourne. If your firm is based in another city, contact us and we will let you know whether we can take on work in your market.

Can a firm operate in more than one of your cities?

Yes. We work with firms that have offices or clients across multiple cities, building coordinated strategies that avoid internal competition between your own pages while maximising visibility in each separate market.

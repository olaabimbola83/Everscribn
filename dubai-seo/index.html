Questions Answered

Do you build separate Arabic and English pages for Dubai clients?

Yes. We build genuinely translated Arabic pages alongside English ones, connected through proper hreflang tags, rather than relying on machine translation. Google can detect thin machine translated content and it will not rank well regardless of the underlying English page quality.

What is DIFC and does it matter for SEO?

DIFC is the Dubai International Financial Centre, a free zone with its own common law legal system used heavily by corporate and financial clients. Firms practising within DIFC benefit from dedicated content addressing its specific jurisdiction, since clients searching for DIFC representation use different terms than those searching for general UAE legal services.

How does real estate law SEO differ from other practice areas in Dubai?

Dubai real estate searches often involve foreign buyers researching from outside the UAE, sometimes months before travelling, which means content needs to address ownership rules, visa linked property investment, and dispute resolution in more depth than a typical local real estate search would require.

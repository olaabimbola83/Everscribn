What Is Law Firm SEO,
and Does It Work?

Law firm SEO is the work that makes your practice findable at the moment someone needs a lawyer. It covers the words on your pages, the structure of your site, your Google Business Profile, and the sources across the web that reference your firm by name.

It works differently from general SEO. Legal search volumes are small, and intent is high. Nobody types “bankruptcy attorney Wilmington” out of curiosity. A Delaware firm ranking for twenty exact terms will out-earn one ranking for two hundred vague ones, so we build around practice areas and counties instead of chasing traffic.

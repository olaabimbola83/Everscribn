Why does mobile page speed matter so much for Lagos law firms?

The majority of legal searches in Lagos happen on mobile devices, often on 3G or 4G connections rather than fast home broadband. A slow loading website loses a large share of visitors before the page even finishes rendering, which is why we prioritise Core Web Vitals and mobile speed above almost everything else for Lagos clients.

Which areas of Lagos do you cover?

We build strategies that account for neighbourhood level search behaviour across Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Lekki, Ikeja, and Surulere, in addition to city wide Lagos targeting, since many prospective clients search by specific area rather than the city as a whole.

Do you write content specific to Nigerian legal terminology?

Yes. Content is written to reflect Nigerian legal terms and procedural references appropriate to Lagos jurisdiction, not generic content adapted from US or UK legal templates.

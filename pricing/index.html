We do not publish fixed price packages because the right investment for a personal injury firm in Lagos is different from the right investment for a corporate law firm in Delaware. We quote after the free audit, when we know what your firm actually needs.

Why no fixed packages

Three Reasons We Quote
After the Audit

🏙️

Cities are different

The competitive investment required to rank in London is materially different from what is needed in Lagos or Delaware. A fixed price package cannot account for this without overcharging one client or underserving another.

⚖️

Starting points vary

A firm with strong local SEO already in place needs a different investment than one starting from zero. We quote based on what we find in the audit, not a standardised tier that ignores your existing position.

🎯

Not every firm needs everything

Some firms need local SEO and nothing else. Others need a full rebuild plus content plus PPC. We do not push services your firm does not need in order to hit a package price point.

What affects your quote

Six Things That
Determine Your Investment

Your city

Competition levels vary significantly between Lagos, London, and Delaware. The investment required to rank reflects what you are actually competing against.

Your practice area

Personal injury requires a heavier investment than estate planning because the competition is more intense and the stakes per click are higher.

Your starting point

A firm starting from zero requires more foundational work than one with existing rankings that just need improving.

Services selected

You can start with one service and add others as results are proven. We do not require firms to purchase everything upfront.

Content volume

The number of practice area pages, city pages, and FAQ articles needed depends on your firm's scope, which we assess during the audit.

Website condition

If your current site needs a technical rebuild before SEO can work, that is a one-time cost quoted separately from the ongoing retainer.

Questions Answered

How long does it take for a business to research and choose a lawyer?

Business clients typically take weeks to months before making contact, often involving multiple decision makers reviewing your website, case history, and credentials. This extended research phase means your content needs to demonstrate deep subject matter authority, not just signal your existence.

Is business law SEO different from consumer legal SEO?

Significantly. Business clients use different search terms, have longer consideration periods, and evaluate authority and expertise more rigorously than individual consumers. Content that works for personal injury or family law performs poorly for corporate audiences who want demonstrated expertise and case history.

Yes. We build dedicated business law SEO strategies for Lagos, Delaware, London, Dubai, and Melbourne, each accounting for the local corporate landscape and jurisdiction specific search behaviour.

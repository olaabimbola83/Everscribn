We work exclusively with law firms no dentists, gyms, or franchise brands. We never take competing firms in your city and practice area. You own everything we build. And we report on signed cases, not just traffic. These aren't bonuses they're the baseline for how legal marketing should work.

How do you make sure the leads we get are actually qualified?

We build keyword strategies around high-intent search terms phrases used by people actively looking to hire a lawyer right now, not just researching. We also review your intake process and can help your team tighten conversion so qualified leads don't fall through after they contact you.

Do you offer AI SEO or GEO for law firms?

Yes, it's built into every engagement now. Clients nowask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews to recommend an attorney before they ever open Google. We structure your content, schema, and citations so those tools cite your firm by name, not just rank it.

What happens to my website and rankings if I leave?

They stay yours. We build on WordPress a platform you own and control. Your content, your SEO authority, your Google Business Profile all of it belongs to you from day one. You never have to start from scratch if you go a different direction.

How transparent are you with what you're spending our money on?

Completely. Every monthly report breaks down what we spent, where it went ad spend vs. content vs. management fees and what it produced. You'll see which channels generated enquiries, which converted, and what your cost per signed client looks like. No bundled mystery fees.

How quickly will we see results?

Most law firms or attorneys see meaningful improvements in qualified enquiries within 60–90 days, primarily from local SEO and Google Business Profile wins. Competitive organic rankings on high-value keywords typically take 4–6 months. We show you progress at every milestone not just in month six.

Do you work with firms in our practice area in other cities?

Yes but never in the same city. Exclusivity is geographic. A personal injury firm in Lagos and one in London can both be Everscribn clients. But we will never work with two firms competing directly for the same clients in the same city.

Results and timelines
How long does law firm SEO take to show results?

Local SEO improvements, particularly Google Business Profile and map pack rankings, typically show measurable results within 30 to 60 days. Competitive organic keyword rankings take 4 to 6 months in most markets, though this varies significantly by city and practice area. We set milestones at 30, 60, and 90 days so you are never left wondering where things stand.

How do you measure success?

We tie reporting to signed cases wherever possible, not just rankings and traffic. Every monthly report shows where your budget went, which channels produced enquiries, and which converted to retainers. Rankings and impressions appear in the report too, but they are context, not the headline.

What results can we realistically expect?

That depends heavily on your city, practice area, and starting point. We will not give you a generic number before we have reviewed your current position. What we can say is that the results on our case studies page, including 127 percent organic traffic growth and 3x lead increases, are real and documented, not best case projections.

Ownership and contracts
Do I own my website and content if I leave?

Yes. Everything we build is yours. Your website is built on WordPress with your credentials from day one. Your content is published on your domain. Your rankings belong to your site. If you choose to leave, you take everything with you and we retain nothing.

Are there long-term contracts?

We do not believe in 18-month lock-ins. We earn your business every month. You own the website and content regardless, so if we stop producing results, you are not trapped paying for a service that has stopped working.

We can audit what your current agency has built and give you an honest assessment. In most cases we can transition smoothly without disrupting existing rankings. The one thing we check first is whether your current agency owns your website, which would require a rebuild before we can do anything meaningful.

Bar compliance
How do you handle bar advertising compliance?

All content is reviewed against the specific advertising rules of your jurisdiction before it is published. This covers testimonials, case outcome references, superlative claims, and any area where rules vary between markets. We review against Nigerian Bar Association rules for Lagos, SRA rules for London, and the relevant rules for each market we operate in.

Can you help with review generation without violating bar rules?

Yes. Most jurisdictions permit soliciting reviews from former clients as long as you do not incentivise them or make specific claims about outcomes. We build review generation systems calibrated to the specific rules of your jurisdiction.

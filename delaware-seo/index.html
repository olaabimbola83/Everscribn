Do you target keywords for both Wilmington and statewide Delaware searches?

Yes. Wilmington carries the highest concentration of corporate legal work, but we also build content and local SEO for Dover, Newark, and statewide searches depending on your practice area and client base.

How is SEO different for business clients compared to individual clients?

Business clients research more thoroughly before choosing counsel, often involving multiple decision makers and a longer consideration period. Content for this audience needs to demonstrate deep subject matter authority and case history rather than relying on urgency driven messaging that works for personal injury or criminal defense searches.

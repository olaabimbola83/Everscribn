FAQ

Personal Injury SEO
Questions Answered

Why is personal injury SEO more expensive than other legal verticals?

Personal injury cases often carry high contingency fee value, sometimes tens of thousands of dollars or more from a single signed client, which means firms are willing to pay aggressively for both SEO investment and paid advertising. That competition drives up the cost and difficulty of ranking compared to lower value practice areas.

How fast do personal injury clients typically search and hire?

Often within hours or days of an accident, not weeks. This compressed timeline means your firm needs to already be visible when the search happens, since there is rarely time to build awareness after the fact the way you might for a slower moving practice area like estate planning.

Do you offer this service in a specific city?

Yes. We build dedicated personal injury SEO strategies for Lagos, Delaware, London, Dubai, and Melbourne, each accounting for the local competitive landscape and the specific compensation schemes or legal frameworks in that market.

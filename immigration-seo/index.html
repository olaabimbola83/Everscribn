FAQ

Immigration SEO
Questions Answered

Why does immigration SEO require multiple languages?

Immigration clients often search in their native language rather than the language of the country they are relocating to, especially in markets like Dubai or London with large expatriate populations. A firm with English only content misses a substantial share of this search volume entirely.

How far in advance do immigration clients typically search?

Often months in advance, particularly for visa categories tied to long term relocation or investment, such as golden visas or skilled worker routes. This gives more time to build awareness through content compared to urgent practice areas like personal injury, but it also means the research phase is longer and more thorough.

Do you offer this service in a specific city?

Yes. We build dedicated immigration SEO strategies for Lagos, Delaware, London, Dubai, and Melbourne, each accounting for the specific visa categories and migration patterns relevant to that market.

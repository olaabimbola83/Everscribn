FAQ

Legal Content
Questions Answered

How much content does our law firm actually need?

It depends on your practice areas and cities served, but most firms need a dedicated page for every practice area and city combination, plus an ongoing flow of supporting articles. We build a content roadmap during your audit so you know exactly what is needed and why.

Will AI generated content hurt our search rankings?

Google does not penalise AI assistance on its own, but it does penalise thin, generic, or unhelpful content regardless of how it was produced. Every piece we publish is researched, fact checked, and edited by someone with legal marketing experience before it goes live.

Do you handle bar advertising compliance review for content?

Yes. Every page and article is reviewed against the bar advertising rules of the relevant jurisdiction before publishing, covering testimonials, case results, and superlative claims.

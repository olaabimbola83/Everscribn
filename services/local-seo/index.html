FAQ

Local SEO
Questions Answered

How many Google reviews does my law firm need?

There is no fixed number, but firms ranking in the top three of the local pack typically have both a higher review count and a higher average rating than competitors in the same city. Review velocity, meaning how regularly new reviews come in, matters more than total volume alone.

Does my law firm need a listing for every office location?

Yes. Each physical office with its own address and staff should have its own Google Business Profile. Listing multiple locations under one profile, or creating listings for virtual offices, violates Google guidelines and risks suspension.

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Local SEO typically moves faster than organic SEO. Most firms see Google Business Profile and map pack improvements within 30 to 60 days, compared to 4 to 6 months for competitive organic keyword rankings.
